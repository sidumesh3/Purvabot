Company History:
Purva Industries was established in 2015 by Mr. Umesh Sancheti, a visionary entrepreneur and son of Mr. Subhaschand Sancheti. Umeshji, along with his wife Ms. Sapana Sancheti, transformed decades of trading experience into a manufacturing legacy.

Their journey began in the 1990s as a humble trading business, rooted deeply in honesty and service to the local farming community. As trust and demand grew, they took a bold step into manufacturing — launching Purva Industries with a focus on quality, consistency, and cattle wellbeing.

What started as a small unit quickly grew into a full-fledged production facility. Due to space constraints and rising demand, the team expanded into a larger unit, preserving their hallmark quality every step of the way.

Today, Purva Industries is a trusted name in Maharashtra’s cattlefeed market — driven by ethics, powered by legacy, and proudly carried forward under the Purva and Gomata brands.
